1 Three directions — same flows, rebuilt

Each direction shows the three screens that carry the product: Home (the one-decision screen), Practice (the core loop, with the listening skill wired in), and Progress (the motivation surface that has to work without streaks). Flow logic and copy rules are unchanged from the build — this is hierarchy, type, texture and metaphor. Pick one and I'll build it out clickable, end to end, including onboarding, stroke tracing and the katakana track.

1a Paper & Ink. Warm paper, one vermillion accent, Instrument Serif. Memory strength is rendered as ink density — a kana starts faint and darkens as you learn it, so progress is legible at a glance with no bars, points or streaks. Practice sits on a genkō-yōshi guide square.
